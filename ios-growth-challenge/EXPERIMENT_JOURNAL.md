## E-001: Opportunity selection (2026-10-03)

**Hypothesis:** the best $1k subscription opportunity sits where users already pay, the incumbents are stale, and organic search competition is thin. The biggest-revenue categories are the wrong place to look.

**Evidence:**
- I screened 26 concepts. Every high-revenue category I looked at (invoice, teleprompter, sobriety, reading, GLP-1) already has a leader with 30k–750k ratings.
- The App Store's #1 result for "ibs tracker" was last updated 2020-10-27. Three of the top 10 results have fewer than 15 ratings.
- 912 negative competitor reviews cluster around surprise charges (41%), missing insight (23%) and data loss (10%). Monash reviews specifically ask for reintroduction guidance.
- Paying users exist: mySymptoms $59.99/yr hard paywall; Fig $69.99/yr.

**Decision:**
- Primary: IBS/low-FODMAP trigger finder with guided reintroduction.
- Backup: migraine trigger tracker on the same engine.
- Pricing: $49.99/yr and $9.99/mo, 7-day trial.

**Experiment:** none yet. Next is kill gate G1 (keyword popularity).

**Cost:** $0.

**Result:** pending.

**Lesson so far:** at category medians, paid search CAC (~$128) is about 2.7× the 24-month LTV (~$48), so paid search alone does not pay back at medians. The business has to win on organic rank and a funnel that beats the medians. Ads are for learning, and for scaling only if CAC falls below about $48.

**Next action:** Human Tasks 1–5. In parallel, write the Phase 2 product spec, the Xcode scaffold and the landing page.
